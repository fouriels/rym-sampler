# The Ultimate RYM Sampler: dashboard and genre atlas

The web companion to [The Ultimate RYM Sampler](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bElxttxaPnc) videos on the
[Musique Non-Stop YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@VirulentHorizon).

[CLICK IT](https://genresampler.com)

- **Dashboard**: facts about the genres: how many releases each has, when each genre's top record came out, where its artist is from, how long it runs, etc.
- **Genre Atlas**: explore the genre tree and listen to each genre's song.

By [Fouriels](https://ko-fi.com/fouriels).

Looking for a specific timestamp? See the full spreadsheet [here](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/13FLYyONZIOSw8dosuoEIWaSq46TqerzwTD-Y0DVRvvE/edit?gid=1637589714).

After scraping [RateYourMusic](https://rateyourmusic.com/) for their genre hierarchy, for the I first chose a cutoff such that every genre with more than 5 albums was included. Representative albums were then chosen such that each album is shown only once. I then used spotify and last.fm to identify the most popular song from that album, and youtube heatmaps to identify the most consequential 7 seconds. After this automated run came the laborious process of manually checking every single song pick to make sure that a) the album/song choices made sense and were appropriate, b) the youtube link was correct, and c) the 7 second clip accurately reflected the genre. 

This specific video is structured such that none of the representative songs repeat themselves - you can listen to the whole thing without hearing the same clip more than once. The other, individual top-level genre videos have more redundancy if you were to listen to them one after the other (e.g 'Electronic Dance Music' and all its sub-genres being in both 'Electronic' and 'Dance'), but are still valuable if you are interested in a particular type of music rather than a wide variety (or otherwise don't want to commit to a 5 hour video).

It would be disingenuous to not mention the fact that this is a snapshot in time, based on an inherently incomplete Western data source on a Western website, and that - on top of the fact that it'll already be outdated in various ways at release - it will inevitably underrepresent some genres (particularly non-Western) of music. Please also be aware that the sheer range of genres and cultures covered means that some viewer discretion may be advised.

While there will be disagreements about what genre a given song 'should' be, or why some genre is absent (in 99% of cases, it's because it wasn't on RYM at the time of the snapshot), the amount of work needed to publish this (and the fact that it was just me working on it) means that this isn't perfect, and there will inevitably be errors - unrepresentative clips, wrong album covers, even mislabeled songs - which will perhaps be something to iterate on if I ever do a v2. For now, there is an errata document [here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1gRl5rXRMRzfIExa1MeDz_wZJPwvJryVMQD4_yWgTHhw/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.tsvnunba0v5l).
